Privacy Policy — Rental Manager

    

Last updated: September 2026

    

        Rental Manager is a property and rental management application.     

    

Data Storage

    

        Rental Manager stores information entered by the user, including         property information, room information, tenant information,         payment records and expense records, locally on the user's device.     

    

Data Collection

    

        Rental Manager does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal         information with the developer or third parties.     

    

        The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or         third-party data collection services.     

    

Data Security

    

        Because information is stored locally on the user's device,         users are responsible for protecting access to their device         and maintaining any backups they require.     

    

Data Deletion

    

        Users can delete information within the app. Deleting the app         may also remove locally stored application data.
